01 / Intellectual compass

Five ways into
the futures conversation.

Connected ideas. Different starting points.
Choose a question worth exploring.

Futures Literacy

Recognising how our assumptions about tomorrow shape what we see and do today.

It creates room for alternatives, helping learners and institutions move beyond a single expected future.

What changes when we imagine more than one tomorrow?
Explore Futures Literacy ↗

Related reading: Pakistan 2035

Strategic Foresight

Exploring change, uncertainty and alternative futures to inform decisions in the present.

Horizon scanning, scenarios and backcasting help institutions test plans before circumstances test them.

Which assumption would make our current strategy fragile?
Explore Strategic Foresight ↗

Related reading: Beyond AI Literacy

Responsible AI

Considering the consequences, limits and responsibilities of how artificial intelligence is designed and used.

AI choices affect people, institutions and opportunities. Human judgment and governance belong in those decisions.

Whose perspective is missing from this AI decision?
Explore Responsible AI ↗

Related reading: The AI Skills Race

AI Literacy

Understanding, evaluating and using AI with an awareness of its capabilities and limitations.

Tool familiarity is only a starting point. Critical judgment and the ability to keep learning matter as tools change.

What should we understand before we trust this output?
Explore AI Literacy ↗

Related reading: Beyond AI Literacy

Future-Ready Education

Learning that combines human judgment, AI literacy and the ability to explore uncertain futures.

Education can help people participate in shaping change, connecting local experience with wider possibilities.
