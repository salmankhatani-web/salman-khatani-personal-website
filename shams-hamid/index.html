About

A philosopher of futures in an age of intelligent systems.

Dr. Hamid’s career connects philosophy, education, technology, foresight and public dialogue. His institutional work helped place Futures Studies, Critical Thinking and Ethics inside university curricula, while his current public scholarship explores how human judgment, uncertainty and responsibility should evolve alongside AI.

Futures

Futures Literacy & Foresight

Designing learning environments that help people challenge assumptions, expand possible futures and make more responsible decisions in the present.

AI

Relational Intelligence

Developing a body of work that shifts the AI conversation from answer production toward judgment, epistemic responsibility, reciprocity and human agency.

Education

Transformative Pedagogy

Building interdisciplinary curricula, digital learning systems and inquiry-based practices across philosophy, ethics, critical thinking and future-oriented education.

Signature Work

Four domains, one question: how do we become more responsible toward the future?

Across these domains, Dr. Hamid’s work forms a coherent intellectual journey from epistemology and education to futures literacy and human–AI governance.

Journey

From philosophy to institutional foresight.

Selected milestones from more than three decades of teaching, research, institutional leadership and futures practice.

1990s

Philosophy, teaching and research foundations

Lecturer at the University of Karachi and later Assistant Professor at Hamdard University. Completed a PhD in Philosophy at Hamdard University in 1999, following first-position degrees in philosophy-related studies at the University of Karachi.

2000s

Education reform and institutional leadership

Worked across teacher development, school leadership, public-sector education projects and university management, including major reform initiatives supported by development partners.

2009–14

University of Toronto collaborations

Worked in academic visitor, research coordination and technology-enabled learning capacities across University of Toronto units, including qualitative research and nursing-related projects.

2019–24

Daren of Social Sciences, Iqra University

Led social-sciences academic development while advancing Futures Studies, Critical Thinking and Ethics in the curriculum and supporting remote-learning infrastructure during COVID-19.

2019/20–present

Founding Director, IMAGINE

Built an institutional platform for Futures Studies and Futures Literacy, public learning, research, international dialogue and applied foresight.

2025–26

Relational Intelligence and global public scholarship

Joined the Lifeboat Foundation Futurists Board, participated in World Futures Day, contributed to a Routledge volume, and developed a sustained essay series on relational intelligence and responsible AI.
