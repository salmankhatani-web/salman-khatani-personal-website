About Dr. Salman Ahmed Khatani

Professional biography of Dr. Salman Ahmed Khatani, a Pakistan-based futurist and academic working in Futures Studies and Foresight, Futures Literacy and responsible AI.

Professional profile

Dr. Salman Ahmed Khatani is a Pakistan-based academic, researcher and futurist working in Futures Studies and Foresight, Futures Literacy, AI Literacy, responsible AI and sustainable development. He serves as an Associate Professor in Business Administration at Iqra University, Karachi. His work connects academic research, teaching, applied foresight and professional education.

His career includes work with IMAGINE: Institute of Futures Studies at Iqra University, strategic foresight projects, university teaching and curriculum development. His Next Generation Foresight Practitioners steering committee profile provides an independently published professional reference.
