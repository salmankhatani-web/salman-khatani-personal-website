AI Literacy

Understanding and using AI matters. The deeper challenge is building capabilities that remain useful as technologies keep changing.

What AI Literacy means here

AI Literacy is the ability to understand what AI systems can and cannot do, use them effectively, evaluate their outputs, recognize limitations and make informed choices about their use. Dr. Khatani's public work treats AI Literacy as necessary but incomplete when technologies, interfaces and workflows keep changing.

Core argument

From tool use to future readiness

Tool-specific competence can expire quickly. A more durable capability stack combines AI Literacy with critical evaluation, Responsible AI, learning agility, Futures Literacy and Strategic Foresight.
