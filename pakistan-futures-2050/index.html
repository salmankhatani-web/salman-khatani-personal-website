Three questions for scenario building

  1. What happens if access to AI expands faster than education and oversight?
  2. What changes if communities and institutions build shared capability and include marginalised perspectives?
  3. Which signals would indicate growing vulnerability, improved resilience or unexpected opportunities?

These are scenario prompts, not predictions, quantified probabilities or national policy recommendations.
