Futures Studies and Foresight in Pakistan

An introduction to Futures Studies and Foresight, horizon scanning, scenarios and Futures Literacy through Pakistani research, education and community practice.

What are Futures Studies and Foresight?

Futures Studies is an interdisciplinary field concerned with how people understand, explore and imagine multiple possible futures. Foresight involves structured approaches for examining changes, assumptions and uncertainties to support decisions in the present. These approaches do not claim to predict a single predetermined future.

Teaching futures from Pakistan

Dr. Khatani's work combines Futures Studies and Foresight with Futures Literacy, higher education, responsible AI and local community engagement. His public research includes a Futures Literacy Lab for climate-related futures and scholarly work on intuition and imagination in scenario planning.
