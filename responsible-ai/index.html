Quick answers about Responsible AI and PAUSE-AI

What does Responsible AI mean in Dr. Khatani's work?

Responsible AI means combining AI capability with human judgment, accountability, awareness of limitations, governance and consideration of social consequences.

What is PAUSE-AI?

PAUSE-AI is an original Responsible AI readiness checklist created by Dr. Salman Ahmed Khatani for Pakistani universities. Version 1.0 was published through Fiker Futures Academy in September 2026. It is a decision framework, not an accreditation standard or certification.

Who is PAUSE-AI designed for?

It is designed for university leaders and educators considering AI initiatives and seeking a structured way to examine readiness, responsibility and institutional consequences before adoption.

Core focus: helping learners and institutions understand AI capabilities and limitations, consider consequences and uncertainty, and make more responsible choices about adoption and use.
