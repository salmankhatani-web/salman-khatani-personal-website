Futures Literacy

Futures Literacy as a capability

Futures Literacy helps people recognize how assumptions and imagined futures influence present decisions. It is not prediction. It develops the capacity to question expectations, explore alternatives and use the future more consciously when making choices today.

How it connects to foresight

Futures Literacy surfaces assumptions and alternative possibilities; Strategic Foresight translates uncertainty into scenarios, signals, choices and action. The two capabilities are complementary rather than interchangeable.
