Future of Work

The future of work is not one forecast. It is a set of possible operating environments shaped by technology, institutions, people and place.

There is no single future of work

AI, automation and organizational change interact with regulation, education, infrastructure, demographics, culture and labour markets. The future experienced by a professional in Karachi may differ significantly from one experienced in London, Nairobi or San Francisco.
